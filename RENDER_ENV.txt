Required neutral tournament-calendar variables for Render:

CALENDAR_ENABLED=true
CALENDAR_BASE_URL=<use the same calendar source base URL you already use>
CALENDAR_REALM=Regular
CALENDAR_REFRESH_MINUTES=30
CALENDAR_DAYS=30
TODAY_ENABLED=true
CALENDAR_MIN_ACTIONS=10

OZY Today now rolls automatically at R+0 = 17:00 UTC.
There is no separate Today posting-time variable anymore.
